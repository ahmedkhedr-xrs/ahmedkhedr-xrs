### 🧭 About Me

I'm a 3rd-year **Information Systems** student who enjoys the full journey a piece of data goes through — from a messy raw extract, to a clean warehouse, to a dashboard someone actually makes a decision with.

- 🔭 I build **end-to-end data pipelines**: ETL, dimensional modeling, and warehousing in **SQL Server**
- 📊 I turn that data into **decision-ready dashboards** with **Power BI** and DAX
- 🌱 Currently sharpening my **Data Engineering** fundamentals as my primary path forward, while staying strong in analysis
- 🧠 I care most about *why* a data model is designed a certain way — not just making a chart look nice
- 💬 Ask me about: star schemas, slowly changing dimensions, Power Query, or DAX measures
